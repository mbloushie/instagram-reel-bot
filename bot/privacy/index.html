Privacy Policy

This is a private, personal tool connected to a single Instagram professional account. It only responds to direct messages from its owner and is not offered to the public.

What data is processed

When the owner sends a direct message or shares a reel or post to the connected account, the app receives the message and media through the Instagram API, plus the sender's Instagram-scoped ID, used only to recognize the owner.

How it is used

Media is downloaded temporarily, turned into still frames and an audio transcript, and sent to an AI service (Anthropic's Claude API) to produce a reply in the same conversation. Temporary files are deleted after processing.

What is stored

Conversation text and short summaries are stored on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold or shared.

Data deletion

Send /reset in the chat, or contact the owner at the email below. Data is deleted within 7 days.
